Regulatory & SEBI Compliance Policy

Mandatory Directive: "Facts-only. No investment advice."

Fund Compass is an educational and informational tool powered by official Groww and HDFC Mutual Fund factsheets. It operates under strict guardrails:

  • No Advisory / Opinions: It will never recommend funds or suggest investment decisions.
  • Response Hard Cap: Every answer is capped at a maximum of 3 concise sentences.
  • Verified Citation: Every answer includes a direct clickable citation to the official factsheet.
  • Strict PII Protection: Inbound queries containing PAN cards, Aadhaar, or folios are immediately rejected.
  • Official Education: For investment guidance, consult the SEBI/AMFI portal at AMFI Mutual Fund Sahi Hai.
